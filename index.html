SMALL STEPS, MORE CLARITY

A useful place for the everyday details.

01

Plan your money

Start with a 50/30/20 split, use expenses and savings, or adjust your allocation. Record multiple incomes and see what is available in each card.

02

Keep bills in view

Add recurring expenses and subscriptions to the card that pays for them. Track due dates, reserve money, and record a payment without counting it twice.

03

Make progress visible

Set several savings goals, review recorded spending, and use a small on-device assistant for supported commands and questions.

YOUR RECORDS, YOUR CONTROL

Local first. Recovery when you need it.

Your ledger stays encrypted on your phone. Optional Google Drive backups are encrypted before upload. Keep the recovery code separately so you can restore records on a replacement phone.

Core records can be used offline with a valid cached entitlement. Purchases, periodic verification, backup transfers and downloads need a connection. Offline voice requires a compatible phone and an installed English speech pack.
