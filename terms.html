3. Three-day onboarding trial

Google sign-in starts or restores one server-issued 72-hour onboarding trial with all Normal and AI features. Trial sign-in is separate from Google Play billing and optional Drive backup consent. Internet is needed to start or restore the trial; remaining access is cached on this phone until the original expiry.

No payment method is requested by TapLedger, no paid subscription is created, and the trial never renews or charges automatically. After it expires, choose Normal or AI if you want to continue paid features. Google Play opens only when you select a paid plan; it displays the actual local price and renewal terms before purchase.

The original trial start and expiry are associated with a server-side keyed hash of your Google identifier. Signing in on a replacement phone with the same Google account returns the original remaining period, not another three days. Financial records require a separate encrypted backup and recovery code.

All supported features are available during the trial, subject to device compatibility. Offline speech needs a compatible Android service and installed English pack; Tavi handles supported English finance commands, not arbitrary tasks.

Deleting trial registration ends access and removes its dates. A limited hashed used-trial marker is retained for 180 days to prevent immediate repeat trials, then removed through routine cleanup. See data-removal instructions. Existing records remain viewable, exportable and recoverable after expiry.

4. Purchase, renewal and changes

Purchases are completed through Google Play using the payment method associated with the purchasing account. The subscription renews automatically for the selected billing period unless you cancel before renewal using Google Play’s controls. Google Play presents the charge and renewal details.

Upgrades, downgrades, billing-period changes, timing of replacements, credits and prorated charges depend on the options shown by Google Play for that change. Review the confirmation before accepting. Prices or terms may change prospectively with notices or consent where required by Google Play and applicable law.

5. Cancellation and refunds

To cancel, open Google Play, choose your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → TapLedger → Cancel subscription. You can also use Google Play’s subscription management page or TapLedger’s Manage / cancel link.

Cancellation normally stops the next renewal while access continues through the paid period, unless a refund, revocation or other store action changes entitlement. Uninstalling the app, disconnecting Drive or requesting data removal does not cancel the subscription.

Refund eligibility depends on Google Play’s policy, the circumstances of the purchase and applicable consumer law. See Google Play’s refund policy or contact support for help. These terms do not exclude mandatory refund or other consumer rights.

6. Restoration and offline verification

On a replacement Android phone, install TapLedger through the same Google Play account that purchased the plan, then choose Settings → Your subscription → Restore purchases. Active access is verified with Google through our service. Internet is required for purchase restoration.

Financial records need a separate surviving encrypted backup and its recovery code. A subscription is not a backup service guarantee. Downloaded command or speech packs may need to be installed again.

Paid features can work with a cached verified entitlement while offline. The current verification window is at most seven days and does not extend past the verified subscription or applicable grace expiry. Reconnect when required. Expired, refunded, revoked, held or unverifiable purchases may lose access to paid features according to their verified status.

7. Your records and plan access

Without an active entitlement, existing records remain viewable and available for supported export and recovery. Adding or changing financial records requires Normal or AI, and command-assistant access requires AI after the onboarding trial when paid access gates are enabled.

Keep a usable encrypted backup and the recovery code separately from the phone. Automatic backups run on a best-effort basis while the app is open. A backup restore replaces the current ledger after confirmation; it does not merge simultaneous edits from multiple devices.

8. Feature limits and responsible use

The assistant is a small English command tool using bounded parsing and deterministic ledger calculations. It can misunderstand or reject wording and should not be relied on as a general-purpose AI adviser. Review confirmations, dates, amounts and assigned cards. If you enable Quick Add, supported clear single expenses may save immediately with an Undo option.

Offline voice requires a compatible on-device speech service and installed English pack. Downloads can require internet, available storage and provider support. Type commands when voice is unavailable. Widget placement, including lock-screen availability, depends on your device and launcher.

TapLedger currently supports one selected currency per ledger. Choose it before adding monetary records. It does not convert existing records between currencies or support mixed-currency accounting.

The app records your manual entries; it does not connect to banks, move money, execute investments, pay your bills or cancel third-party subscriptions. A bill marked paid is a bookkeeping entry, not proof that a provider received payment.

9. Budgeting guidance and service availability

Budgets, savings suggestions, affordability estimates and reports are based on the records you provide. They may omit unrecorded expenses or obligations. They are general planning information and do not replace regulated financial, investment, tax or legal advice. No savings outcome is guaranteed.

We work to keep supported functions available, but cannot promise uninterrupted access or compatibility with every phone or third-party provider. Services can be affected by store, network, storage or platform changes. We may update features or discontinue a service with notice where practicable and as required by law.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, you are responsible for reviewing your entries and maintaining backups. Nothing in these terms limits liability or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
