1. Restore your subscription

  1. On the replacement phone, use the same Google Play account that purchased TapLedger.
  2. Install TapLedger from Google Play and connect to the internet.
  3. Open Settings → Your subscription → Restore purchases.
  4. If access is missing, check your plan in Google Play subscriptions, confirm the purchasing account and contact support with the transaction reference if needed.

Restoration recovers remaining verified paid access. It does not start a new paid period or restore your financial records.

2. Restore income, expenses, bills and goals

  1. Choose Restore from Google Drive, or choose a previously exported encrypted backup file.
  2. For Drive, connect the Google account that holds the backup.
  3. Select the archive and enter its original recovery code or password.
  4. Review the replacement warning before confirming. Export any existing ledger you want to keep first.

You recover records only up to the successful backup selected. We cannot recover a lost local-only ledger or decrypt an archive without its recovery code. Google Play purchase restoration cannot replace those requirements.

3. Set up offline voice

In Assistant, choose Set up voice. Request the English pack, or open the phone’s Voice input / Offline speech recognition settings and install English. Finish the download, grant microphone permission, and try again. On older supported Android versions, installing through system settings may be required.

Error 13 means the requested speech language is currently unavailable, often because its pack is not downloaded. Android may support a different installed English variant. The app checks available variants where the platform supports that check.

The speech pack is separate from TapLedger’s command pack. Offline voice requires Android 12 / API 31 or later and a compatible on-device speech service. Downloading a pack may need internet; subsequent recognition remains on-device. If the provider cannot supply a compatible pack, type your command.

4. Wallets, budget cards and widgets

A money wallet manually tracks where money lives, such as cash, a bank account or mobile money. A budget card tracks what that money is planned for. Creating a wallet does not create a login or connect to a bank.

Add the Android widget from your home screen’s Widgets picker or from TapLedger Settings on supported launchers. Add and Ask open the app and respect device/app locking. Lock-screen placement depends on the phone. If the launcher shows an old preview after an update, remove and re-add or resize the widget.

5. Remove your data

There is no separate TapLedger sign-in account to close. Local records, cloud backups, exported files, support messages and purchase metadata need separate removal steps.

Keep any recovery copy you need first.

Deleting the only surviving ledger or backup can permanently remove those records. Canceling payment and deleting data are separate actions.

From this Android phone

Open Android Settings → Apps → TapLedger → Storage and choose the option to clear app data, or uninstall TapLedger. Menu names vary by phone. This removes the local working ledger and app storage under the phone’s controls; it does not erase exported files, Drive backups or records held by Google Play.

From Google Drive

Turn off automatic backup in TapLedger first so a new archive is not uploaded. In Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → TapLedger → Options → Delete hidden app data, where available. You can also disconnect the app or revoke its Google account access. Revoking access alone does not necessarily remove existing archives. See Google’s hidden app-data instructions.

From exported or shared files

Delete backup archives and CSV files from Downloads, Files, email, external drives or any destination where you saved or shared them. Other recipients control their copies. Remove your separately saved recovery code only when you no longer need any archive encrypted with it.

From support and purchase verification

Email sadzanrahin1998@gmail.com with the subject TapLedger data removal request. Tell us whether your request concerns support messages, purchase-verification records, or both. We may ask for minimal information needed to locate the record and confirm ownership.

Verification records currently require manual removal. Limited information may need to remain for applicable legal, fraud-prevention or transaction-dispute reasons. We will explain what can be removed and any relevant retention exception. A later purchase restoration can recreate verification information needed to restore paid access. We cannot delete Google’s own transaction records, access a backup without your authorization, or remotely erase a ledger on your phone.

Never email your recovery code, Google password, full card details, service-account keys or an unredacted financial database. Redact unrelated information in screenshots.

6. Cancel a subscription or request a refund

Use Google Play’s subscription management page, or open Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → TapLedger.

Uninstalling TapLedger, deleting data or disconnecting Drive does not cancel a subscription. Refunds depend on Google Play’s refund policy and applicable consumer rights. Read the subscription terms for billing and access details.

7. Send a useful support request

Include the app version, phone model, Android version, what you tried and what happened. For a purchase issue, include the plan and an appropriate transaction reference if needed; redact unrelated payment information. For recovery issues, describe the backup source and error without sending the recovery code.
