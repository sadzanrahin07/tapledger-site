Privacy policy

A clear explanation of what stays on your phone, what is transmitted, and the choices you have.

Updated 28 October 2026Operator: Sadzan Rahin
Your ledger is stored locally.

Optional backups are encrypted before upload to your Google Drive. Purchase verification and support requests use separate data flows, explained below.

1. Who operates TapLedger

TapLedger is developed and operated by Sadzan Rahin (“we”, “us” or “our”). This policy covers the TapLedger Android app and its public introduction, privacy, terms and support pages.

Contact: sadzanrahin1998@gmail.com. Google sign-in starts or restores the optional onboarding trial. It is separate from Google Play purchases, optional Google Drive authorization and manually tracked money wallets. Trial sign-in does not upload your ledger or grant general Drive access.

2. Financial records on your phone

Information you enter—including your optional name, income, expenses, manually tracked wallets, budget allocations, bills, savings goals and preferences—is stored in the app’s encrypted local database. Database keys are kept in the phone’s platform secure storage.

We do not upload your readable ledger to a TapLedger server to provide budgeting or on-device commands. Confirmed commands can create or change local ledger records. Plaintext CSV exports and any copies you share are controlled by you and are not protected by the app’s database encryption once exported.

3. Optional Google Drive backup

When you choose to connect Google Drive, Google’s sign-in software handles your selected Google account and authorization credentials. The app requests the drive.appdata permission to create, list, download and rotate its own encrypted backup files in the app-specific folder. It does not request general access to all your Drive documents.

The archive is encrypted on your device before upload. A recovery code or password is needed to decrypt it. The recovery secret is not uploaded alongside the backup and is not sent to our purchase-verification service. If you enable automatic backup, the recovery secret is kept in this phone’s secure storage to encrypt later backups.

Automatic backup is best-effort while the app is open; it is not continuous multi-device sync. The app normally rotates its own backups to retain the five most recent. You can revoke Google access and remove stored app data using Google’s controls. Revoking access alone may leave existing backups in Drive.

Information received from Google APIs is used for the backup and recovery features you request, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. On-device commands, microphone and widgets

The assistant processes supported English commands and ledger queries on your device. The current command pack is a small intent model with bounded parsing and validation. It does not send your commands or ledger to a cloud language model.

Microphone permission is optional. When you tap the microphone, explicitly start a “Hey Tavi” session, or tap the widget’s Speak action and unlock the app, Tavi requests on-device speech recognition through Android’s speech service. These are short foreground sessions; there is no background or always-on listening. Recognition stops when the app leaves the screen. Saying the wake phrase only works during an explicitly opened session. Raw recordings are not intentionally saved by TapLedger or uploaded by TapLedger to a cloud recognizer. Supported financial changes are previewed before confirmation. If you explicitly enable Quick Add, clear single expenses can save immediately with Undo. Data saved as an entry becomes part of the local ledger. Optional spoken replies use an installed offline English text-to-speech voice.

Offline voice depends on the phone, its speech provider and an installed English language pack. The provider may need internet to download or update that pack. A TapLedger command-pack download also needs a connection; its host receives standard connection information, not your ledger. Typing is available when offline voice is unavailable.

Widgets contain Add and Speak to Tavi launch actions. Speak opens the assistant and starts a foreground voice session after unlocking; it does not record from the background. They do not display ledger balances, financial entries or recordings. Android’s device lock and any configured TapLedger app lock apply before you continue.

5. Google Play and subscription verification

Google Play handles purchase checkout, payment details and subscription management. TapLedger does not receive your full payment-card number or Google account password.

To verify access, the app sends the purchase token, app package and subscription product identifier to our verification service over HTTPS. The service checks the purchase with Google and may acknowledge it. Google also sends subscription lifecycle notifications to this service.

The verification database keeps a hashed purchase identifier and entitlement information such as product, plan tier, status, expiry and last verification time. The raw purchase token is used for the verification request and is not intentionally stored in the entitlement database.

Our verification service and its hosting provider receive standard network information, such as the request’s IP address and time. The service uses transient request information for security and rate limiting; hosting infrastructure may maintain operational logs. Ledger records are not part of these verification requests.

6. Google-linked trial registration

If you choose the three-day trial, Google sign-in obtains identity information and an ID token for your selected Google account. The app sends the ID token over HTTPS to our service. The service verifies the signature, intended audience and verified account claims with Google software before granting access. The token can contain your email and Google identifier, but we do not intentionally persist the token, raw Google identifier or email in the trial database.

The trial database stores a keyed hash of the stable Google identifier and the original trial start and expiry. This allows restoration on another phone and prevents restarting the trial by reinstalling. No ledger records, voice recordings or payment-card details are stored with the trial. Trial dates are retained to preserve the original entitlement until you request deletion.

You can delete the registration from Settings → Your subscription → Delete trial registration. This removes its dates and ends trial access. For 180 days we retain only a keyed used-trial marker and its deletion deadline to prevent immediate repeat trials. Expired markers are removed by routine hourly cleanup and on server startup. Support can help with verified deletion requests if the app is inaccessible. Deleting registration does not delete Google’s account or Play transactions, cancel subscriptions, or remove local records and Drive backups.

7. Support, permissions and website visitors

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and, where necessary, verify ownership for a purchase or data-removal request. Please avoid sending financial records unless they are necessary and redact unrelated information.

The app may request microphone permission for voice, notification permission for optional bill reminders, and device authentication for app locking. Device authentication is performed by the operating system; TapLedger does not receive your fingerprint or face template. File pickers and sharing tools access only the files or destinations you select.

This website includes no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or third-party font requests. Its fonts and styling are served as local site assets. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Clicking an email or external link opens a separate service with its own practices.

8. How information is used and shared

We use the information described above to provide the features you request, secure the service, verify paid access, restore purchases, respond to support and comply with applicable obligations.

TapLedger includes no advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs in the current app. We do not sell your personal data or use your ledger to target advertisements. We do not use your private ledger or voice recordings to train a shared model.

Information may be processed by Google for Play and Drive, by the services hosting purchase verification or command-pack downloads, by GitHub for this site, and by the email provider handling support. Those providers receive information needed for their respective roles. They may process information in countries outside your own. A user-directed export or share sends a copy to the destination you select.

We may disclose limited information where required by a valid legal obligation, to address fraud or security issues, or to protect lawful rights. Applicable privacy rights and required safeguards continue to apply.

10. Security and recovery

We use an encrypted local database, platform secure storage, encrypted backup archives and HTTPS for supported network requests. These measures reduce risk; no device or online service can guarantee absolute security.

Keep your phone secure and retain the recovery code outside the phone. We cannot decrypt a backup without the recovery secret or recover a ledger that has no surviving backup. Restoring a purchase restores paid access, not financial records.

11. Children, changes and contact

TapLedger is a personal budgeting service and is not designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address it.

We may update this policy as features, providers or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised policy and date on this page and provide additional notice where required.
